Facet Software is a South African company building fast, careful tools for clinicians working in public hospitals. It is run by a practising medical doctor.

01 — Our work

DeltaLabiPhone

A native viewer for laboratory results held in the National Health Laboratory Service (NHLS) lab portal. Clinicians sign in with the NHLS credentials they already hold, find a patient by name, and read results in seconds.

DeltaLab is an independent product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHLS, and it is a viewer for laboratory data, not a diagnostic device.

02 — How we build
  1. iPatient data stays on the device.We run no servers that hold patient information. Results travel between the clinician’s phone and the laboratory, and nowhere else.
  2. iiNo analytics, no tracking, no advertising.Our apps collect nothing about the people who use them.
  3. iiiBuilt on the ward.Every feature starts from a real problem on a real ward round, and is tested there before it ships.
